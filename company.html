Director’s message · Sustainability

Better industry.
Shared responsibility.

“Pakistan’s industrial progress should be measured not only by what we produce, but by how responsibly we use the resources entrusted to us.”

For manufacturers in Pakistan, sustainability must make sense on the factory floor as well as in the boardroom. Energy costs, material losses and supply uncertainty make efficiency a commercial necessity. Environmental responsibility and business resilience must move forward together.

At DuPlast, our ambition is to help customers make better material decisions: choosing grades suited to their application, reducing avoidable waste and considering durability and recyclability alongside performance and cost. A material’s value extends beyond its purchase price to how it performs throughout its useful life.

International credibility is earned through evidence, not broad promises. We aim to strengthen sourcing transparency, technical documentation and traceability, and support customers in understanding the quality, safety and environmental requirements of their intended markets. Any claim about recycled content, performance or compliance should be supported by appropriate documentation and testing.

We currently serve more than 100 customers, from small businesses to larger manufacturers. Our commitment is to make responsible progress practical at every scale—combining Pakistan’s entrepreneurial strength with the discipline expected in international supply chains. That is the business we want to build, and the future we want to help shape.

Muhammad YousafDirector & Owner · DuPlast (SMC-Private) Limited
